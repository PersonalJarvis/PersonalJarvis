# Personal Jarvis — Agent Mode

## Style Prompt

A precise, music-led product film. One launch task unfolds inside the actual Agents interface. Large editorial statements alternate with carefully cropped interface footage. Camera motion guides attention to a prompt, an agent handoff, or the final answer, then stops to let the audience read. The island and real conversation renderer supply the visual identity; retain their native colors.

## Colors

Canvas #0e0d0c; surface #191815; primary ink #f7f7f4; secondary ink #c2bdb0; accent #e7c46e; hairline #48443b. These follow the existing warm charcoal/gold brand. Original product captures retain their own palette.

## Typography

Inter 400/500/600 for all editorial text. Inter bold only for the two-word feature name. Main headlines 88–116px, supporting text 30–38px, indexed labels 22px. UI is enlarged through targeted crops. 96px horizontal safety margin. No serif imitation of the reference brand.

## Motion

30 fps. Functional camera reframes 0.65–1.0s with power3.inOut; arrivals 0.5–0.8s with power3.out; mask reveals 0.55s. Preserve the product pointer and actual recorded clicks. Use clean cuts between meaningfully different views, and short 0.25s crossfades only between title and product. Holds last long enough to read. Never loop breathing effects.

## What NOT to Do

- No copied reference audio, logos, or commercial footage in the final video.
- No invented application controls or live-performance claims.
- No generic neon, particles, glass cards, or stock technology montage.
- No redesign of the world, characters, or app.
- No bouncing text, long logo intro, tiny full-screen UI, or excessive captions.
