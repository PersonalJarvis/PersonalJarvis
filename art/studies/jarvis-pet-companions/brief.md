# Jarvis pet companions (Jarvis Verse)

## Intent

The person picks a desktop pet in **Settings → My Pets** (`docs/pets.md`). In
the Jarvis Verse office map, Jarvis keeps the person company as a small
companion behind their character. Until now that companion was always the 3D
Gigi model. This study replaces it with a 3D version of the chosen pet, so the
pet the person sees on the desktop is the one that walks, crawls or flies
behind them in the world.

## References

The only references are the built-in pixel pets in `jarvis/ui/pets/builtin/`
(generated by `scripts/pets/build_pets.py`, project-owned). Silhouette, palette
and personality come from their idle rows; no external assets are used.

| Pet | 3D reading | Moves by |
|---|---|---|
| `gigi` | The existing Gigi hover companion (`art/studies/gigi-hover-companion`) | flying |
| `ember` | Chibi baby dragon: red scales, cream belly bands, peach wings, yellow horns | flying, wings beating |
| `miso` | Chibi orange tabby: big head, cream muzzle and paws, pink ears, green eyes | walking on four legs |
| `bolt` | Blue battery with a dark charge window, three green bars, grey cap, nub arms and feet | waddling on two feet |
| `brew` | Teal teapot with a gold knob, spout and handle, a face on its belly | hopping |
| `mochi` | Pink jelly blob | squash-and-stretch bouncing |
| `shelly` | Green snail with an orange spiral shell and eyes on stalks | crawling |

A pet the person drew themselves (a single uploaded sheet) has no authored
model. It is extruded in the runtime into a voxel figure from its own idle
frames and floats low, so every choice still shows in 3D.

## Look

- Matches the office's toy style (`docs/agent-society/office-map.md` §2):
  chunky rounded primitives, flat matte colours, dot eyes with one glint, no
  textures. Colours are taken from the pixel pets' palettes.
- Blender master: metres, Z up, -Y forward (glTF +Z forward), bottom-centred
  pivot per pet. Animated parts hang under named pivot empties
  (`<pet>_Head`, `<pet>_Leg_FL`, `<pet>_Wing_L`, …) so the runtime can drive
  them; names use underscores because three.js strips dots from node names.
- Motion is procedural in the runtime (`components/society/companion/petRig.ts`),
  synced to real ground speed, so legs never slide. Reduced motion freezes
  every gait and ambient loop.

## Budgets

- Whole pet GLB under 400 KB, under 30k triangles, no textures.
- One pet on screen at a time (the person's companion); one draw call per
  part, no lights of its own for ground pets.

## Review

Runtime review in the office map at the normal follow view, close up and in the
overview, in light and dark app appearance (the diorama keeps one palette).
